About Me

I create animated worlds, characters & visual stories.

Animation student • 3D & motion artist — endlessly curious about movement, emotion and the frames in between.

My work moves between 2D and 3D — from hand-drawn loops and storyboards to modeled, lit and rendered scenes. I love the moment a static drawing first starts to breathe, and I'm learning everything I can about how timing, spacing and small details bring emotion to the screen.
